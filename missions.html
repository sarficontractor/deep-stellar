Having surveyed Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune, these legendary twin probes are now in interstellar space, bearing the Golden Record as humanity’s message to the stars.

INFRARED DEEP VISION · 2021-PRESENT

James Webb Space Telescope

Stationed at Lagrange Point 2 (L2), JWST peers through cosmic dust to capture the earliest galaxies formed just hundreds of millions of years after the Big Bang.

  • MIRROR SIZE6.5 Meters (Gold Beryllium)

LUNAR TO DEEP SPACE · 2022-FUTURE

The Artemis Program

Humanity’s return to the Moon, landing the first woman and next person of color, establishing the Gateway space station, and preparing for the first human expedition to Mars.

  • LAUNCH VEHICLESpace Launch System (SLS)
  • SPACECRAFTOrion + Human Lander
  • OBJECTIVEPermanent Lunar Base
SATURN & TITAN EXPLORER · 1997-2017

Cassini-Huygens

A thirteen-year orbital odyssey around Saturn that uncovered liquid methane lakes on Titan, active water geysers on icy Enceladus, and concluded with the dramatic Grand Finale dive.

  • ORBITS OF SATURN294 Completed
  • SURFACE LANDINGHuygens Probe on Titan
  • LEGACYOcean Worlds Discovery
MARS SURFACE ROVERS · 2020-PRESENT

Perseverance & Curiosity

Advanced nuclear-powered science laboratories actively traversing Martian craters, seeking ancient biosignatures, collecting rock cores, and testing oxygen production via MOXIE.

  • LOCATIONJezero Crater, Mars
  • SPECIAL COMPANIONIngenuity Helicopter
  • MISSION GOALAstrobiology & Sample Cache
OUTER OCEAN PROBE · 2024-FUTURE

Europa Clipper

Targeted to perform nearly 50 close flybys of Jupiter's icy moon Europa to determine whether its vast subsurface ocean possesses conditions suitable to sustain extraterrestrial life.

  • LAUNCH DATEOctober 2024
  • TARGET ARRIVAL2030 (Jupiter System)
  • INSTRUMENTSIce-Penetrating Radar
