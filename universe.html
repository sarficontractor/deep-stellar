SPIRAL GALAXY · 2.5M LIGHT-YEARS

Andromeda (M31)

Our majestic celestial neighbor, Andromeda contains over one trillion stars and is moving toward the Milky Way at approximately 110 kilometers per second for an eventual cosmic merger in 4.5 billion years.

  • DIAMETER220,000 Light-Years
  • STELLAR MASS~1.5 Trillion M☉
  • CONSTELLATIONAndromeda
GRAND DESIGN SPIRAL · 23M LIGHT-YEARS

Whirlpool Galaxy (M51)

Renowned for its dramatic spiral arms and gravitational dance with companion galaxy NGC 5195, M51 is one of the premier laboratories for studying galactic structure and intense star formation.

  • DIAMETER76,000 Light-Years
  • MORPHOLOGYSA(s)bc pec
  • CONSTELLATIONCanes Venatici
UNLENTICULAR GALAXY · 31M LIGHT-YEARS

Sombrero Galaxy (M104)

Famous for its brilliant white bulbous core and dark, absorbing dust lane encircling its rim like a wide-brimmed Mexican hat, Sombrero harbors a supermassive black hole exceeding 1 billion solar masses.

  • DIAMETER50,000 Light-Years
  • BLACK HOLE MASS1 Billion M☉
  • CONSTELLATIONVirgo

GRAVITATIONAL ANOMALY

Dark Matter

An invisible, mysterious substance that does not emit, absorb, or reflect light, yet accounts for roughly 85% of all matter in the cosmos and holds galaxy clusters together.

✦ 27% of Total Universe
ACCELERATING EXPANSION

Dark Energy

A repulsive energy permeating all space, driving the accelerating expansion of the universe and outbalancing the mutual gravitational pull of cosmic matter.

✦ 68% of Total Universe
SPACETIME SINGULARITY

Supermassive Black Holes

Regions of infinite density where gravitational forces are so intense that nothing—not even light—can escape. At the center of almost every large galaxy sits one of these monsters.
