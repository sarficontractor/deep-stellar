Worlds of Fire and Ice.

A closer look at the diverse conditions across the inner and outer systems.

INNER ROCKY WORLD

Mercury

The smallest and innermost planet, with extreme temperature swings ranging from 430°C in sunlight to -180°C in deep shade, racing around the sun in just 88 Earth days.

  • ORBITAL PERIOD88 Days
  • SURFACE TEMP-180°C to 430°C
  • MOONS0
GREENHOUSE CAULDRON

Venus

Earth's scorching twin possesses a crushing atmosphere of carbon dioxide and clouds of sulfuric acid, creating a runaway greenhouse effect making it the hottest planet.

  • ORBITAL PERIOD225 Days
  • SURFACE TEMP465°C
  • ATMOSPHERE96.5% CO₂
THE BLUE MARBLE

Earth

The only known haven for life in the cosmos, equipped with active plate tectonics, abundant liquid water oceans, and an oxygen-rich nitrogen atmosphere shielded by a powerful magnetosphere.

  • ORBITAL PERIOD365.25 Days
  • WATER COVERAGE71%
  • MOONS1 (The Moon)
THE RED FRONTIER

Mars

A desert world with dusty iron-oxide soil, polar ice caps, and Olympus Mons—the solar system's tallest volcano. Evidence shows Mars once had lakes, rivers, and a denser atmosphere.

  • ORBITAL PERIOD687 Days
  • SURFACE TEMP-65°C Avg
  • MOONS2 (Phobos & Deimos)
THE GAS TITAN

Jupiter

More massive than all other planets combined, Jupiter is a gas giant with swirling atmospheric belts, the Great Red Spot storm, and more than 95 known orbiting moons.

  • ORBITAL PERIOD11.86 Years
  • GREAT RED SPOT>350 Years Old
  • MOONS95+ Moons
JEWEL OF THE SYSTEM

Saturn

Adorned with thousands of dazzling ringlets made of water ice and rocky debris, Saturn is also home to Titan, a moon with rivers and lakes of liquid methane.

  • ORBITAL PERIOD29.4 Years
  • RING SYSTEM WIDTH282,000 km
  • MOONS146 Moons
THE TILTED ICE GIANT

Uranus

Rotating nearly sideways at a 98-degree axial tilt, Uranus exhibits extreme 42-year seasons of continuous sunlight and darkness above an icy mantle of water, ammonia, and methane.

  • ORBITAL PERIOD84 Years
  • AXIAL TILT97.77°
  • MOONS28 Moons
THE SUPERSONIC REALM

Neptune

The most distant major planet, enveloped in vivid azure clouds driven by supersonic winds reaching 2,100 km/h, orbiting in the shadowy twilight over 4.5 billion km from the Sun.

  • ORBITAL PERIOD164.8 Years
  • WIND SPEEDSUp to 2,100 km/h
  • MAJOR MOONTriton (Geysers)
